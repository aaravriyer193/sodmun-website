Platform

Entering a new era.

Since SODMUN IV, every committee has run on its own platform. Chairs and delegates manage POIs without paper and keep committee organised from the first speech to the final vote.

  • Direct messages between delegates and a clean line to the chairs, so no note gets lost across the room.

  • Blocs get a group chat and send Points of Information as messages. Chairs see and manage every POI in one place.

  • A built-in assistant for procedure questions, research pointers and quick clarity mid-session.

  • A shared timer for speeches and caucuses that everyone in committee can see.

  • Draft, share and review working papers and resolutions without printing a page.
